Changelog This release backports GHSA-fx35-mq7g-6g98 (Verification bypass via public key in legacy bundle) to Cosign v2.6.x. We strongly encourage folks to continue their migration to the bundle format. The Cosign v3.1.x releases support both formats; the primary change being that the default for signing is the bundle format (although you can specify --new-bundle-format=false to sign with the old format).
